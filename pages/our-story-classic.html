Our story

Founder. Engineer. Visionary.

Qress-ent was founded by Engineer Ojiambo Catherine Juma, but long before it became a movement, it was an instinct born in childhood.

“I remember I was very, very passionate about beauty and hair since I was young,” Catherine recalled in an interview. “I naturally knew how to do hair. I started doing my sister’s hair at only seven years old.”

The long way home.

01

A crown to heal.

That intuitive gift met its hardest test when she turned twelve. A battle with chronically dry scalp, stubborn dandruff, and hair that refused to grow chipped away at her self-esteem. Product after product failed, and conventional salons only worsened the frustration. The turning point arrived after high school: following a stint with temporary locs, she made a quiet vow to stop handing her hair over to outside stylists and learn to heal it herself. Throughout university, she nurtured her own crown, discovering firsthand the transformative power of patient, informed care.

02

Qressinah Enterprises.

That breakthrough turned into a business. Alongside her twin sister, Tracy, she launched Qressinah Enterprises. It started small and scrappy. Tracy mastered nails, Catherine styled hair, and together they formulated and retailed liquid shampoo.

03

Two and a half years.

For two and a half years, the business grew, until Catherine moved to Italy to pursue her postgraduate studies. The distance closed the salon and opened something else.

04

Italy.

Thousands of miles away from home, she couldn’t find kinky textured extensions, pure rosemary oil, or sulfate-free formulas. Living in Europe made her fall deeply in love with the continent she had left behind. She saw clearly how so many Africans had been conditioned to abandon their own rich heritage to blend into Western aesthetics. Embracing Afrocentric hairstyles abroad became her quiet rebellion and creative awakening.

05

Home.

Realizing that everything she needed to revive and honor ancestral haircare was waiting back home, she returned. She transformed the modest beginnings of Qressinah Enterprises into Qress-ent: a brand built on ancestral research, clean green science and one plain belief: African hair is not a problem to correct.

Hair is identity.

Our mission is to reconnect Africans globally with their identity, heritage and confidence by unearthing ancestral hair care wisdom, providing accessible education, and creating natural solutions that allow African hair to thrive.

We want to be the first place anyone looks for African hair knowledge, so that every African can wear their natural crown with pride, clarity and ease.

Meet the twins.

Catherine’s journey is inseparable from her twin sister, Tracy. Their shared bond is etched into the very roots of the brand: long before Qress-ent existed, their partnership began in childhood play and grew into Qressinah Enterprises, a name born from the fusion of their nicknames, Qatrinah and Reece. It was a space built on mutual trust, where Tracy mastered nail artistry while Catherine tended to crowns.

In African heritage, twins represent a sacred balance: harmony, duality, and an intertwined spiritual destiny. Tracy was Catherine’s very first client and muse at just seven years old, sitting patiently as small hands learned an ancestral craft, and she remains her client and fiercest supporter today. As the enduring face of the brand, Tracy grounds the vision in real sisterhood.

That partnership is still the heartbeat of Qress-ent: ancestral memory on one side, modern ideas on the other, and care that starts at home.
