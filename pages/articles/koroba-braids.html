Koroba Braids

Koroba braids turn the crown into living sculpture — structured rows finished with shells or beads, beauty and protection in the same craft.

Koroba braids sit within the wider family of West African and diaspora braid artistry: clean parts, intentional bulk, and finishes that announce pride.

Form and function

Like other protective styles, Koroba keeps ends tucked from daily friction while creating a bold silhouette. Cowries and beads are not afterthoughts; they complete the geometry.

Wearing them today

Whether for ceremony or everyday elegance, Koroba braids ask for skilled hands and a scalp that is clean and moisturised. The result is heritage you can wear to work, to celebration, to the mirror.
