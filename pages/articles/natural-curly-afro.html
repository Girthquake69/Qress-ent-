Culture & Stories

The Natural Curly Afro

The afro is one of Africa’s most powerful visual languages — coils and curls as freedom, dignity and belonging.

Long before it became a global icon of the 1960s and 70s, the natural afro existed as everyday African hair left to grow in its own pattern.

Continent and diaspora

On the continent, full natural textures have always been present — shaped with combs, clays and care. In the diaspora, the afro also became a political statement: a refusal of forced assimilation.

Significance now

To wear a natural curly or coily afro today is to honour a texture that grows from African genetics and history. It needs moisture, protection and respect — not apology.
