Ubuntu

Radical Inclusivity

Nguni (Zulu/Xhosa) · Southern Africa

“I am because we are”

Ubuntu affirms that every African hair texture belongs. Coils, curls, kinks, locs, braids, afros — all are valid expressions of identity. There is no single standard of beauty. Qress-ent celebrates the full spectrum of African hair and the people who wear it with pride.
