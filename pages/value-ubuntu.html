Nguni (Zulu/Xhosa) · Southern Africa

Ubuntu affirms that every African hair texture belongs.

Coils, curls, kinks, locs, braids, afros: all of it is identity. There is no single standard of beauty. Every texture and every person who wears it belongs here.
