Our Story

FROM QRESSINAH TO QRESS-ENT.

A cultural wellness movement founded by twins Catherine and Tracy Ojiambo to reconnect Africans with ancestral hair wisdom and identity.

Twin founders. One vision.

Qress-ent is founded and run by twin sisters Catherine and Tracy Ojiambo. Their shared vision sits at the intersection of ancestral anthropology, clean green science and modern digital education.

Previously known as Qressinah Enterprises, the brand evolved into Qress-ent — a clearer expression of purpose: to treat African hair not as a problem to be solved, but as a sacred canvas of identity.

Hair is identity.

Our mission is to reconnect Africans globally with their identity, heritage and confidence by unearthing ancestral hair care wisdom, providing accessible education, and creating natural solutions that allow African hair to thrive.

We envision becoming the definitive global authority on African hair care and cultural preservation — sparking a generational cycle of knowledge where every African embraces their natural crown with pride, clarity and autonomy.

The Qress-ent philosophy

“Hair is an identity.”

Qress-ent is not a cosmetics company. It is a cultural tech and wellness movement. We reclaim lost knowledge (Sankofa), pass it across generations (Elimu), celebrate every texture (Ubuntu), keep care simple and accessible (Kimuntu), and protect cultural integrity (Ase).

Unlike approaches that treat 4C and textured hair as a problem, we see African hair as a sacred canvas of identity, heritage and power.
