Our Story

FROM QRESSINAH TO QRESS-ENT.

A journey rooted in African hair, culture and the shared vision of twin founders.

Twin founders. One vision.

Qress-ent is run by twin founders Catherine Ojiambo and Tracy Ojiambo. Their shared vision is deeply connected to African hair, hairstyles, roots, culture and identity.

The company was previously known as Qressinah Enterprises. Qress-ent is the evolution of that foundation — a clearer expression of the brand’s purpose and the belief that sits at its centre.

Hair is more than appearance.

Hair can carry culture, heritage, identity, memory, beauty, confidence and expression.

At Qress-ent we believe African hair is not merely something to maintain or style. It is part of who we are — a living expression of roots, tradition and self.

This belief shapes every product, every story and every conversation we have with our community.

The Qress-ent philosophy

“Hair is an identity.”

We create with African hair in mind. We celebrate the diversity of textured hair, protective styles, and the cultural stories that travel with them. Our goal is to offer care that feels personal, authentic and rooted in heritage.
