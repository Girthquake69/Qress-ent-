Sankofa

Reclaiming Lost Knowledge

Akan · West Africa

“Go back and fetch it”

Sankofa teaches us to look back so we can move forward. At Qress-ent we recover ancestral African hair knowledge that was lost, hidden or erased — techniques, plants, rhythms of care, and the wisdom that once travelled freely across generations. We bring that knowledge into the present so it can serve our hair and our identity again.
