Our Journey

FROM QRESSINAH TO QRESS-ENT.

Founded by twins Catherine and Tracy Ojiambo, Qress-ent is a cultural wellness movement sitting at the intersection of ancestral anthropology, clean science and modern education.

We exist to reconnect Africans globally with their identity, heritage and confidence — unearthing ancestral hair care wisdom so every natural crown can thrive with pride.

Meet Catherine & Tracy

The Founders

MEET THE TWINS.

Catherine and Tracy Ojiambo are the twin founders of Qress-ent. Their shared vision is to reconnect Africans with ancestral hair wisdom, identity and confidence.

What began as Qressinah Enterprises has grown into a cultural wellness movement — one that treats African hair as a sacred canvas of heritage, not a problem to be solved.
