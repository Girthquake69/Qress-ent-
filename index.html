Our Journey

FROM QRESSINAH TO QRESS-ENT.

Founded by twins Catherine and Tracy Ojiambo, Qress-ent grows from a deep appreciation for African roots, hairstyles and the stories carried by our hair.

The brand is built around a simple belief: hair is not merely something we wear. It is part of identity, culture, beauty and self-expression.
