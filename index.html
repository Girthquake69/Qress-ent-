Where we started

From Qressinah to Qress-ent.

Engineer Catherine Juma started Qress-ent with her twin sister Tracy. It grew from a small hair and beauty business into a brand that studies where African hair practice comes from and teaches it forward.

We dig up old hair care wisdom, test it against what we know today, and share it plainly, so every natural crown can grow with pride.
