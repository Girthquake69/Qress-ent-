STEPS AFTER THE SITE IS ONLINE
------------------------------
1. Deploy this folder to your host (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, etc.)

2. Google Search Console
   - Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Add property: qressent.shop
   - Verify ownership
   - Submit sitemap: https://qressent.shop/sitemap.xml
   - URL Inspection → Request indexing for https://qressent.shop/

3. Test the thumbnail (after deploy):
   - https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/
